## Context

What forces this decision. Include the constraint, the requirement or the problem, the relevant
parts of the current system, and anything that limits the option space (product requirements,
operational limits, team size, existing decisions). State facts, not preferences. Link to
[`../product-spec.md`](../product-spec.md) or an earlier ADR where the constraint comes from
there.

## Decision

The decision, stated in the present tense as a rule the codebase must follow. Be specific
enough that a reviewer can tell whether a pull request complies. Include the concrete
mechanisms (packages, configuration, enforcement) where they are part of the decision.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier, safer or cheaper.

### Negative

- What becomes harder or more expensive, and what risk is accepted.

### Neutral

- Facts that follow from the decision without being good or bad, including obligations it
  creates.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative name

What it is, and why it was rejected. One paragraph is enough. Rejections must give a reason
tied to the context, not a preference.

### Another alternative

Why it was rejected.
